## What it does

A flash-attention forward kernel in Mojo for video DiT self-attention shapes on the AMD RX 7900 XTX (gfx1100): non-causal, no mask, f16, head dimension 128, layout `[L, H, D]`, with a parity harness against an fp64 reference.
